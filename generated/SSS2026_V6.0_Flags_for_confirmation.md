# SSS2026 ILR — V6.0 flags for confirmation (22 September 2026)

V6.0 is the production infrastructure around the finalised prototype: the recipient register, the runner, the served (chunked) package, the checks, the publication commands and the Azure estate. Nothing in a report changed (the three V5.1 schools rebuilt through the runner: 0 states moved). The flags below are the questions the round raises for the translator, the report owner, Sport Wales and operations; nothing here is decided by Industryline. Each is also on Framework v2.13 sheet 73 where it concerns the workbook.

## 1. Translator

**Three loading-mask frames (sheet 43, Framework v2.13).** `ui.loading_view` "Loading this view…", `ui.load_error` "This view could not be loaded. Check your connection and try again.", `ui.retry` "Try again". Shown only on the served package while a view's data is fetched; marked English in Welsh mode until returned.

**Four local-authority rows on sheet 53 in the dataset's spelling.** The dataset names the authorities `Carmarthenshire`, `Conwy`, `Rhondda Cynon Taf` and `The Vale of Glamorgan`; sheet 53 has no `Carmarthenshire` row and spells the other three `Conwy County`, `Rhondda Borough` and `Vale of Glamorgan`. 211 of the 935 eligible schools are in these four authorities. The client's proper-name lookup shows the English value silently in Welsh mode when a row is missing (`nameOf`, since v2.3), so without the rows those reports would carry an English authority name on the Welsh cover and profile. Four rows (English exactly as the dataset spells it, Welsh as the translator rules — `Rhondda Borough` looks like a mis-entry for Rhondda Cynon Taf and should be checked) — or the owner's instruction to map the dataset spellings onto the existing rows.

**Still pending from earlier rounds:** the fourteen `ui.alt_yac_*` alt texts and `ui.a11y_switch_desc` (V4.16/V4.17), `ui.a11y_glossary_heading` (V4.15), the four sheet-22 qualifier rows (V5.0: `ov_none_reported`, `et_other_grouped`, `tp_prefer_not_to_say`, `tp_communication_aids` — held cohorts at real schools until returned).

## 2. Report owner

**Schools with a gap in their responded year groups (58 held).** `ui.overview_note` and `ui.faq_included` say "All year groups from Year {first} to Year {last} are represented" / "Pupils in Years {first} to {last}". Fifty-eight eligible schools have at least one year group with no accepted response inside their range (for example Years 3, 5 and 6 but not 4), so the sentence would be untrue. Options: a frame variant that lists the year groups ("Year groups 3, 5 and 6 are represented"), or a rule that the range is stated only when it is complete. Either is a workbook edit (English on sheet 49, Welsh from the translator) and the 58 schools are then built under the tag that carries it. The register status is `held_profile_gap`; the reason names the missing years.

**The three English loading-mask strings** (above) — confirm or amend.

**Schools with 5–13 accepted responses — decided (owner, 22 Sep 2026): they receive a report.** They are eligible under the rule of five (a whole-school view of five is reportable); every year-group view is suppressed, so the report is thin. Thirty such schools are in the eligible set; the register's minimum stays at 5.

**Special schools (16).** Built under the profile family their responded years imply (primary / secondary / combined) and flagged `special` in the register. The review's profile-matrix point (10.2): confirm this treatment, or a distinct profile.

**Slug and file naming.** Production slugs carry the school id (`new-inn-primary-school-6782320`) so that two schools with the same name never collide; the token in the link is unrelated to the slug.

**D69 and EN-08** as before.
